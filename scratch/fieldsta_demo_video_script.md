# Fieldsta Demo Video — Script

**Target length:** ~3:20–3:50. Pulled word-for-word from the live site (fieldsta.com and /try) as of today — re-check before recording if you've shipped copy changes since.

**On the dashboard walkthrough:** kept this to a brief 15-20s glimpse (Scene 10) rather than a full settings tour — this video's job is proving it works fast to a cold prospect, not onboarding someone who already signed up. A full walkthrough also risks whatever real client/campaign data is sitting in your account on camera. If you want the full tour instead, that's genuinely a separate, second video aimed at people post-signup, not this one.

## Notes for recording

- **Don't fake the wait in Scene 5.** The 20–40 second real-agent delay is a selling point in the copy itself ("not a lookup") — cutting it undercuts your own pitch. Speed it up in editing after, if needed, rather than skipping it live.
- **Let the verdict/reasoning text actually load before reading it** — it's a live agent call, so the exact wording will vary run to run. Read what's actually on screen, not this script verbatim, for Scenes 6 and 9's bracketed lines.
- **Keep the browser at a normal zoom/width** so the score ring, criteria list, and draft card aren't cramped — this content reflows narrower on small viewports.
- **Scene 10 (real dashboard):** whichever account you log into, glance at what's actually in the Inbox queue before recording — if it's got a real client's name, email, or lead details visible, either blur it in editing or pick a moment/account where the visible rows are generic enough not to matter. Don't read PII out loud either way.
- Current pricing on the page is **$500/mo Starter** (what Scene 12 references) — the Growth ($1,500) and Scale ($3,000) tiers exist on the pricing section if you want a scene for them, but the /try page and this script both point viewers at the $500 pilot as the entry point.
